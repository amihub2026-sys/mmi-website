Training Guidelines

Arrive Early

Reach the academy 10–15 minutes before class for check-in and preparation.

Stay Hydrated

Bring a water bottle and drink regularly before, during and after training.

Wear Sports Gear

Use clean, comfortable training clothes. Coaches will advise on discipline-specific equipment.

Train Clean

Maintain personal hygiene, trimmed nails and clean gloves or protective equipment.
