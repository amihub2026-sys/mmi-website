mma FIGHTING TRAINING ACADEMY WEBSITE

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. For best results, use VS Code Live Server.

PAGES
- index.html
- about.html
- programs.html
- schedule.html
- gallery.html
- contact.html

CUSTOMIZATION
- Replace phone, address and email inside assets/js/components.js and contact.html.
- Replace image URLs with your academy photos.
- Main colors are located at the top of assets/css/style.css.
- The contact forms currently show a successful demo alert. Connect them to your backend, Formspree or email service for real submissions.

FEATURES
- Responsive header and mobile menu
- 3-slide automatic hero slider
- Scroll reveal animations
- Animated counters
- Programs and pricing cards
- Trainer profiles
- Testimonial slider
- Interactive schedule tabs
- Gallery lightbox
- FAQ accordion
- Contact form
- Mobile padding and responsive grids
