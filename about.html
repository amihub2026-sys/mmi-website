Who We Are

Where Discipline Creates Complete Fighters

MII Fighting Training Academy is a professional combat-training centre offering MMA, KUDO, Boxing, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Karate, Taekwondo, Kenjutsu and Krav Maga under one roof.

Whether your goal is fitness, practical self-defence, personal confidence or competitive fighting, our structured coaching helps you progress with correct technique, discipline and safety.

  • Ten martial arts disciplines
  • Beginner to competition-level training
  • Fitness and practical self-defence
  • Safe and disciplined training environment

Our Mission

At MII Fighting Training Academy, our mission is to provide professional training across multiple martial arts disciplines while developing fitness, self-defence, confidence and competitive ability in every student.

To build MII Fighting Training Academy into a trusted centre for martial arts excellence where beginners, children and aspiring fighters can achieve their highest potential.
